Tips for a strong request

  • Say what you want to achieve, and by when.
  • Say where you are now: your role, your skills, or your product's stage.
  • Name your limits: time each week, budget, location.
  • Add specifics: job links, company names, numbers, what you've already tried.
  • Attach what helps: a CV, job post, offer letter, pitch deck, product doc, spreadsheet or screenshots. Paste links, such as a LinkedIn profile, portfolio, prototype or code repository, in the description.
